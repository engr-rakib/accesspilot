## The "Wow" Features

### Create Roles in Minutes, Not Meetings

Build a role around a job, not a person. Choose a name like "Support Desk" or "Auditor." Then tick off the pages and features that role should see. **One screen. Full picture. Zero guesswork.**

### Feature-Level Precision

Not just "can they open the page?" — can they open the page, use the tool, and press the button? You control it all, down to individual actions. Need someone to view reports but not change them? **A few ticks makes it so.**

### Add People to Roles in Seconds

Someone joins the support team? Drop them into the role. Someone leaves? Take them out. **Their access updates instantly** — no waiting for an administrator, no chasing forgotten accounts.

### Every Feature, Cataloged for You

The whole platform is laid out in one clear, organized tree. Every page, every card, every button — visible at a glance. **You'll never wonder whether something is protected. You can see it and control it.**
